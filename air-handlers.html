Applied Air Handling Units (AHU)

Engineered for low leakage, superior acoustic attenuation, and configurable modularity. From compact mechanical room retrofits to 100,000 CFM hospital surgical cleanroom air handlers.

Vision® Indoor Air Handling Units

Vision air handlers offer patented double-wall injected foam panels with continuous thermal breaks to prevent condensation. Complete component flexibility includes direct-drive fan arrays, antimicrobial drain pans, UV-C lights, and silencers.

Models: CAC / CAH
Airflow: 900 to 100,000 CFM
Insulation: 2" or 4" R-13 Polyurethane Foam
Leakage: AHRI 1350 Class 1 Rating

PreciseLine® Compact Air Handlers

Designed specifically for tight equipment rooms and ceiling closets where custom units cannot pass through standard 36-inch doors. Delivers full commercial-grade double-wall construction and low-vibration ECM direct drive fans.

Models: BCH / BCV / BCA
Airflow: 600 to 10,000 CFM
Orientation: Horizontal & Vertical Floor Mount
Motor: High-Efficiency ECM / VFD Driven
