Engineering Better Air for a Sustainable Tomorrow

SAS-CAN Services designs, manufactures, and services advanced commercial and industrial HVAC systems for the world's most demanding buildings.

Our Heritage & Mission

Pioneering Applied HVAC Engineering

For decades, SAS-CAN Services has been at the forefront of thermodynamic innovation. From introducing the first commercially viable oil-free magnetic bearing centrifugal chiller to developing variable-volume ratio screw compressors, our solutions lead the industry in reliability and lifecycle efficiency.

We operate state-of-the-art ISO 9001 certified manufacturing facilities and engineering test laboratories across North America, backing every product with an extensive network of factory direct technicians and stocking parts depots.
