Decarbonizing Commercial Built Environments

Heating and cooling account for roughly 40% of commercial building carbon emissions. SAS-CAN applied solutions empower building owners to reach Net Zero targets through electrification, heat recovery, and ultra-low GWP refrigerants.

Four Pillars of SAS-CAN Decarbonization

Achieving meaningful sustainability without sacrificing human comfort requires an integrated mechanical engineering approach.

1. Electrification & Commercial Heat Pumps

Replacing fossil-fuel boilers with all-electric air-source and water-source heat pumps. The Rebel Heat Pump rooftop unit operates in reverse refrigeration heating down to 0°F (-18°C) ambient.

Explore Rebel Heat Pumps

2. Simultaneous Heating & Cooling (Heat Recovery)

Don't reject heat to the atmosphere. SAS-CAN Templifier® centrifugal water heaters capture waste condenser heat from chillers and boost it to 160°F for domestic hot water and perimeter heating with a COP of 5.2+.

Explore Templifier Systems

3. Next-Gen Low-GWP Refrigerants

We are leading the industry transition in compliance with the AIM Act, phasing down legacy HFCs in favor of R-32, R-454B, and R-513A, achieving up to 75% reduction in Global Warming Potential.

Read Low-GWP Guide

4. SiteLine™ Intelligent Energy Telemetry

Continuous cloud-connected monitoring eliminates energy drift. Automated algorithms detect dirty coils, stuck economizer dampers, and unneeded compressor staging before energy bills escalate.

Explore SiteLine IoT
Corporate Commitment

Our Net Zero Vision 2050

SAS-CAN Services is committed to reducing greenhouse gas emissions across our entire product lifecycle by 50% by 2030, and reaching Net Zero carbon emissions by 2050 through circular manufacturing, low-carbon refrigerants, and ultra-high efficiency applied engineering.
