Core Rooftop Technologies

Inverter Compressor

Dynamically matches thermal load across 25% to 100% speed, preventing energy-wasting on/off cycling.

Energy Recovery Wheels

Total enthalpy wheels recover both sensible and latent heat from exhaust air to pre-condition 100% outside air.

Native BACnet IP

MicroTech IV controls communicate seamlessly with all major BMS platforms without expensive proprietary gateways.
