High-Efficiency Applied Chiller Portfolio

From oil-free magnetic levitation to variable-volume ratio screw compressors, SAS-CAN chillers provide the world's highest part-load efficiencies and whisper-quiet operation for enterprise infrastructure.

Zero Friction Magnetic Levitation

Magnitude® Centrifugal Chillers

Magnetic bearing technology eliminates mechanical contact, lubrication oil pumps, and seal replacements. Magnitude chillers maintain their factory design efficiency over their entire operating life with zero oil degradation.

Models: WMC, WME, WMT
Tonnage: 75 to 1,500 Tons
Acoustics: Down to 58 dBA
Refrigerants: R-134a, R-513A (Low GWP)
Download Catalog Request Selection
Variable Volume Ratio (VVR)

Pathfinder® Air-Cooled Screw Chillers

Pathfinder chillers feature variable volume ratio (VVR) technology and factory variable frequency drives (VFD) that automatically adjust internal compression volume to match real-time outdoor lift conditions, delivering unmatched part-load IPLV ratings.

Model: AWV
Tonnage: 175 to 565 Tons
Fans: High-Static EC Aero-Fans
Controls: MicroTech® IV with Cloud IoT
Technical Submittal Contact Specialist
Compact & Redundant

Trailblazer® Air-Cooled Scroll Chillers

Designed for rooftop installations with limited structural capacity, Trailblazer chillers feature an all-aluminum microchannel condenser coil, rapid restart technology in under 35 seconds, and dual independent circuits for mission-critical redundancy.

Model: AGZ-F / EWYQ
Tonnage: 10 to 240 Tons
Restart: Rapid Restart < 35 sec
Coils: Microchannel Anti-Corrosion
